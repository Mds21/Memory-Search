# Memory-Search

## Objective

Memory-Search lets people find anything in their personal collection of photos, videos and documents just by describing it in everyday language, instead of digging through folders and file names.

It reads and understands every file it is given: the text inside PDFs, Word files, PowerPoint decks and spreadsheets, the words in scanned cards and pictures, what a photo shows and where and when it was taken, and what is said or shown in a video. So a request like "share my PAN card", "beach photos from Goa" or "what does my rental agreement say about the deposit?" brings back the right file, or the answer from it, in seconds, privately, on the person's own server.
